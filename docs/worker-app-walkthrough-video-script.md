# Worker App Walkthrough Video Script

Use this as the recording script for a short worker training video.

## Video Goal

Show a worker how to start the day, open a work order, capture field updates, and finish the day without needing a laptop.

## Suggested Length

3 to 5 minutes.

## Recording Flow

1. **Intro**
   - Show the worker login page.
   - Say: "This is the mobile worker app used for clocking in, viewing assigned jobs, capturing photos, recording materials, and completing the daily ticket."

2. **Sign In**
   - Enter worker email and password.
   - Tap **Sign in**.

3. **Clock In**
   - Show the shift clock bar.
   - Tap **Clock in**.
   - Explain that field actions require an active clock-in.

4. **Open Jobs**
   - Open the **Jobs** tab.
   - Use the calendar strip to select today or another date.
   - Tap a work-order card.

5. **Review The Work Order**
   - Point out the work order number, status, service, scheduled date/time, property address, route button, and checklist count.

6. **Move Through Workflow**
   - Tap **Travel**, **On site**, **Work**, **Photos**, and **Done** as the job progresses.
   - Mention that each tap is recorded in the timeline.

7. **Photos And Receipts**
   - Open the photo action.
   - Show choosing before/after/issue photo or receipt.
   - Mention captions and that multiple uploads are supported.

8. **Materials And Notes**
   - Mark a planned material as used.
   - Add a worker note.
   - Mention voice dictation can be used from the device keyboard where available.

9. **Activity**
   - Open **Activity**.
   - Start and end an office/supplier/shop/travel activity.
   - Explain that Activity is for non-billable work or non-property time, and it appears on the Day Ticket.

10. **Loadout**
    - Open **Loadout**.
    - Show tools/equipment assigned for the day and return tracking.

11. **Ticket**
    - Open **Ticket**.
    - Show the Day Ticket preview.
    - Explain that operations sees the same ticket.

12. **Clock Out**
    - Return to the shift clock.
    - Tap **Clock out**.
    - Close with: "If a job, route, tool, or checklist looks wrong, contact operations before continuing."

## Production Notes

- Record in mobile viewport first, then optionally include a short tablet view.
- Use a training worker account and sample work orders.
- Avoid showing real owner emails, phone numbers, or private addresses in the recording.
- Keep the audio direct and practical; workers should be able to follow it while standing on site.
