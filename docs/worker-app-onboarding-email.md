Quick start:

1. Open the worker app on your phone or tablet.
2. Sign in before your shift starts.
3. Tap **Clock in** when your work day begins.
4. Open **Jobs** and select your assigned work order.
5. Follow the workflow: **Ready**, **Travel**, **On site**, **Work**, **Photos**, **Done**.
6. Add before/after/issue photos and receipts when needed.
7. Record materials used or emergency purchases on the job.
8. Add worker notes for anything operations or the owner should know.
9. Use **Activity** for non-billable work or non-property time such as office, supplier, shop, or general travel activity.
10. Use **Loadout** to check tools/equipment and confirm returns.
11. Review **Ticket** before the end of the day.
12. Tap **Clock out** when your shift is finished.

Important:

- Do not start work-order actions unless you are clocked in.
- If another activity is active, close it before starting a work order.
- If you cannot complete a checklist item, add a note and contact operations.
- If a job is missing or scheduled incorrectly, contact operations instead of creating a duplicate record.
